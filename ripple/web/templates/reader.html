Writing a script

A marker states what a line is and is dropped when the line saves. Without one, the line follows what came before it: under a cue or a parenthetical is dialogue, everywhere else is action.

Note

A heading typed on the write-here line starts the next scene. An all-caps line reads as a cue; write a line under it that is not a speech and it settles into action.
