INT. TRANSPLANT SUITE - NIGHT

A steel flask goes into a padded case. A gloved hand seals
it and writes a time on the lid in marker: 23:41.

                      SURGEON
          Seven minutes of margin. Not eight.

EXT. HOSPITAL LOADING BAY - NIGHT

MATÍAS REYES, 30s, courier, takes the case two-handed and
puts it in the footwell of a white van.

The van has a magnetic decal on the door and a dent in the
offside wing that has been there a while.

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

Matías clips the case in. Starts the engine. A dashboard
light stays orange and he ignores it.

                      DISPATCH (V.O.)
          Bridge is shut. You're going round.

                      MATÍAS
          Round is nine minutes.

                      DISPATCH (V.O.)
          Then round is what you've got.

EXT. RIVER ROAD - NIGHT

The white van comes out of the bay fast and turns away from
the river.

Sodium light, wet tarmac, no other traffic.

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

Matías drives with the heel of one hand. Checks the case.
Checks the clock.

The passenger door opens at thirty miles an hour.

A WOMAN swings in and pulls it shut behind her. Late 40s.
Wet coat. No bag.

                      THE PASSENGER
          Keep driving.

                      MATÍAS
          Get out.

                      THE PASSENGER
          At this speed?

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

                      MATÍAS
          There's a hospital at the other end
          of this.

                      THE PASSENGER
          I know what's in the box.

EXT. UNDERPASS - NIGHT

The van goes under. Strip lights strobe across the
windscreen.

INTERCUT - VAN AND CONTROL ROOM

INT. AMBULANCE CONTROL ROOM - NIGHT

BÉLA VARGA, 50s, dispatcher, watches a slow green dot on a
map.

                      BÉLA
          Reyes, you have a second heat
          signature in that cab.

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

                      MATÍAS
          I'm aware.

                      BÉLA (V.O.)
          Do you want me to call it in?

Matías looks at the woman. She looks back.

                      MATÍAS
          Not yet.

END INTERCUT

EXT. MARKET STREET - NIGHT

Barriers. A closed street. The van brakes hard and the tyres
let go on wet cobbles.

STUNT: the van slides sideways into a line of empty crates
and stops.

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

The case has come out of its clip. Matías gets it back in
with one hand.

                      THE PASSENGER
          You're four minutes down.

                      MATÍAS
          Five.

EXT. MARKET STREET - CONTINUOUS

The offside wing of the white van is crumpled back into the
tyre. The old dent is gone under a new one.

The wheel rubs. It will keep rubbing.

OMITTED

EXT. CANAL PATH - NIGHT

Too narrow for the van and he takes it anyway. Bollards go
past a hand's width from the mirrors.

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

                      THE PASSENGER
          My daughter is on that list.
          Further down than whoever this is.

                      MATÍAS
          That isn't how it works.

                      THE PASSENGER
          I know precisely how it works.
          That's the problem.

EXT. CANAL PATH - NIGHT

The rubbing tyre starts to smoke. A thin grey line follows
the van along the water.

INT. AMBULANCE CONTROL ROOM - NIGHT

Béla watches the dot slow.

                      BÉLA
                (into headset)
          Reyes. You're losing speed.

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

                      MATÍAS
          I've lost a tyre.

                      BÉLA (V.O.)
          How far?

                      MATÍAS
          Two streets. Maybe.

EXT. CANAL PATH - NIGHT

The tyre goes. The van drops onto the rim and grinds to a
stop with sparks coming off the offside.

INT. WHITE VAN - CONTINUOUS

Silence. The engine ticks.

Matías unclips the case.

                      THE PASSENGER
          Give it to me.

                      MATÍAS
          No.

                      THE PASSENGER
          I'm faster than you.

He looks at her. She is not lying.

EXT. CANAL PATH - NIGHT

Matías gets out with the case. Starts to run.

The woman gets out the other side and runs with him.

EXT. CANAL STEPS - NIGHT

Sixty steps up. He takes them two at a time and loses ground
anyway.

She reaches the top first and holds a hand down.

EXT. HOSPITAL APPROACH - NIGHT

They come out onto a road with the hospital lit at the end
of it.

Neither of them is running any more. Both of them are still
moving.

INT. AMBULANCE CONTROL ROOM - NIGHT

Béla stands up to see a screen she does not need to stand up
to see.

                      BÉLA
          He's on foot.

EXT. HOSPITAL APPROACH - NIGHT

                      THE PASSENGER
          Ninety seconds.

                      MATÍAS
          Then take it.

He holds the case out. She does not take it.

EXT. HOSPITAL APPROACH - CONTINUOUS

                      THE PASSENGER
          If I carry it I'll want to drop it.

She takes his elbow instead and pulls him forward.

INT. HOSPITAL ENTRANCE - NIGHT

Automatic doors. A trolley team waiting. A nurse takes the
case and it is gone.

The clock over the desk reads 23:47.

INT. HOSPITAL CORRIDOR - NIGHT

Matías puts his back against the wall and slides down it.

The woman stands where she stopped. She does not sit.

INT. HOSPITAL CORRIDOR - CONTINUOUS

                      MATÍAS
          What's your name?

                      THE PASSENGER
          Ilona Nagy.

                      MATÍAS
          Your daughter's on the list.

                      ILONA
          Two hundred and eleven.

EXT. CANAL PATH - DAWN

The white van on its rim, offside wing crumpled, one tyre
gone to canvas.

A recovery truck backs up to it in the grey light.

INT. AMBULANCE CONTROL ROOM - DAY

Béla writes 23:47 in a log and underlines it once.

She writes a second line below it and does not underline
that one.

FADE OUT.
